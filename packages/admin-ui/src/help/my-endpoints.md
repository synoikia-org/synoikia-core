The endpoints that your role lets you reach.

## Connect a client

1. Make a token under My profile.
2. Add the address of an endpoint to your MCP client, with that token.
3. Approvals of your calls open in your browser.

## Endpoints you cannot reach

Your role can let you see other endpoints by name. Ask an administrator for access to one of them.

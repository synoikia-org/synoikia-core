The connection of an endpoint to its upstream service: the address, the credentials and the status of the last sync.

## Catalog

The catalog is the list of operations that the plugin found on the upstream. A sync reads it again. New operations take the level of their group.

## Setup notes

The plugin gives these notes. They say what the upstream needs, such as an API key and its rights.

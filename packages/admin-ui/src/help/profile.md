Your account, your sign-in and your own tokens.

## Tokens

A token lets an MCP client act for you, within your role. Copy it when you make it. It is not shown again.

## Two-factor authentication

An authenticator app adds a code to your sign-in. Keep the recovery codes in a safe place.

## Approval browsers

A browser that you trust opens approvals without a new sign-in.

One endpoint as your role lets you use it.

## Your own levels

Your role sets the most that each operation can do here. Where the role allows it, you can lower a level for your own clients. ↺ on a group goes back to the level of your role.

Off means that your clients cannot call the operation. An administrator can give your role more.

## How an admin sets the levels of an endpoint {admin}

An admin sets the levels of the endpoint on its Access page. The tab of a role sets the most that the role can do.
